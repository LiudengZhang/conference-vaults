# SARC041 — Abemaciclib vs placebo, advanced dedifferentiated liposarcoma

> **Abstract:** LBA2 · **Session:** Plenary, Sun May 31, 2026, Hall B1 · **Presenter:** Mark A. Dickson, MD (Memorial Sloan Kettering) · **Embargo:** lifted 7:00 AM CT May 31

## At a glance

- **Sponsor:** Investigator-initiated (SARC cooperative); funded by Eli Lilly
- **PI / presenter:** Mark A. Dickson (MSK)
- **NCT ID:** NCT04967521
- **Phase:** 3
- **Design:** Randomized, double-blind; abemaciclib vs placebo
- **N:** 108 (recurrent or metastatic dedifferentiated liposarcoma)
- **Primary endpoint(s):** PFS — **met**
- **Comparator:** placebo
- **Indication:** advanced dedifferentiated liposarcoma (DDLPS), not amenable to surgical resection
- **Line:** advanced/metastatic

## Headline result

**Median PFS 9.7 vs 1.5 months; ~62% reduction in risk of progression or death (HR ≈ 0.38).** This is the **first-ever positive phase 3 in dedifferentiated liposarcoma** — a subtype with poor prognosis, high relapse, and no prior systemic standard.

## Mechanism / class

Abemaciclib is a CDK4/6 inhibitor. DDLPS is characterized by *CDK4* amplification (12q13–15 amplicon), providing a direct biologic rationale for CDK4/6 blockade.

## Discussant takeaway

Framed as establishing a new systemic option / potential standard of care in a setting with essentially no effective drugs; the very short placebo-arm PFS (1.5 mo) underscored the unmet need.

## Practice-changing?

**Likely yes** for the DDLPS subset — first randomized evidence of benefit; positions abemaciclib as a new standard for unresectable/advanced disease.
