## Headline result
Syndax reported in its pre-meeting announcement that the oral presentation will describe *favorable* overall survival and relapse outcomes among 21 adults and children with KMT2Ar, NPM1m, or NUP98r acute leukemia who received revumenib as post-transplant maintenance, benchmarked against a historical cohort of the same genetic subtypes treated before revumenib became available. *Specific OS, relapse-rate, HR, and p-value figures were not released pre-meeting and are embargoed until the Jun 2, 2026 presentation* (*per Syndax topline, May 21, 2026*).

## Mechanism / class
Revumenib (Revuforj) is an oral, first-in-class menin inhibitor that disrupts the menin–KMT2A protein interaction driving leukemic transcriptional programs in KMT2A-rearranged and NPM1-mutant leukemias. FDA-approved for relapsed/refractory acute leukemia with a KMT2A translocation (adult and pediatric ≥1 year) and for relapsed/refractory AML with a susceptible NPM1 mutation (adult and pediatric ≥1 year with no satisfactory alternative).

## Discussant takeaway
Not reported pre-meeting; omit pending presentation.

## Practice-changing?
Pending. Post-transplant maintenance is a genuine unmet need in menin-dependent acute leukemias, but this is a small (N=21), historically-controlled experience rather than a randomized trial. Hypothesis-generating; the randomized phase 3 newly-diagnosed programs (EVOLVE-2, REVEAL-ND) will drive any practice change.
