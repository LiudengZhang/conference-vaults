# frontMIND — Tafasitamab + lenalidomide + R-CHOP, 1L high-risk DLBCL/HGBL

> **Abstract:** LBA7000 · **Session:** Oral Abstract Session — Hematologic Malignancies: Lymphoma and Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia, Sat May 30, 2026, 3:00–6:00 PM CT (4:00–7:00 PM ET) · **Presenter:** Georg Lenz, MD (University Hospital Münster, Germany) · **Embargo:** May 30, 2026

## At a glance
- **Sponsor:** Incyte
- **PI / presenter:** Georg Lenz, MD (University Hospital Münster, Germany); co-authors incl. M. Trněný, J.M. Burke
- **NCT ID:** NCT04824092
- **Phase:** 3 (randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, global)
- **Design:** Tafasitamab + lenalidomide + R-CHOP vs placebo + R-CHOP, 6 cycles, then tafa+len maintenance
- **N:** 899 adults, age 18–80 (tafa-len-R-CHOP n=448; R-CHOP n=451)
- **Primary endpoint(s):** Investigator-assessed PFS (ITT)
- **Comparator:** R-CHOP + placebo
- **Indication:** Newly diagnosed high-intermediate / high-risk DLBCL or high-grade B-cell lymphoma (HGBL); IPI 3–5, or aaIPI 2–3 if ≤60 yrs
- **Line:** First-line (1L)

## Headline result
At a median follow-up of 35.2 months (data cutoff Oct 20, 2025), frontMIND met its primary endpoint: investigator-assessed PFS favored tafasitamab + lenalidomide + R-CHOP over R-CHOP alone, with **HR 0.75 (95% CI 0.59–0.96; P=.0194)** — a 25% reduction in risk of progression or death. Median PFS was not reached in either arm; the absolute PFS benefit was 8.2% at 2 years (24-month PFS 71.1% vs 62.9%) and 6.6% at 3 years (36-month PFS 67.3% vs 60.7%). The benefit was deeper in the centrally confirmed DLBCL/HGBL subset (n=773; HR 0.68, 95% CI 0.52–0.88). The key secondary endpoint of event-free survival was also met (HR 0.79, 95% CI 0.64–0.97; P=.0260; 24-mo 65.0% vs 56.7%). Interim OS favored the triplet but did not reach significance (HR 0.85, 95% CI 0.63–1.14; P=.2703; 82 deaths [18.5%] vs 97 [21.7%]; 24-mo 84.1% vs 80.5%). CR/ORR were similar between arms. Safety: grade ≥3 TEAEs 86.7% vs 76.1%, most commonly anemia (22.8%), thrombocytopenia (13.1%), and neutropenia (12.4%), with no new safety signals; treatment discontinuation 5.2% vs 5.4%; R-CHOP dose intensity preserved in both arms.

## Mechanism / class
Tafasitamab (Monjuvi/Minjuvi) is a humanized, Fc-engineered anti-CD19 monoclonal antibody, already FDA-approved with lenalidomide for relapsed/refractory DLBCL not eligible for transplant (L-MIND). frontMIND moves the CD19 antibody + immunomodulatory backbone into the first-line setting on top of standard R-CHOP.

## Discussant takeaway
Discussant flagged that the magnitude of OS benefit remains immature and that more data are needed on how prior tafasitamab (anti-CD19) exposure interacts with subsequent CD19-directed CAR T-cell therapy at relapse. Trade-press coverage framed the triplet as a potential "new standard" for newly diagnosed high-risk DLBCL/HGBL, the first regimen in years to beat R-CHOP on PFS in this population.

## Practice-changing?
Pending. First positive frontline phase 3 regimen to add to R-CHOP and improve PFS in high-risk DLBCL/HGBL in this design, and Incyte plans a 2026 sBLA filing for the 1L indication. But median PFS was not reached, OS is not yet significant, grade ≥3 toxicity is higher, and unresolved CAR-T sequencing questions temper immediate standard-of-care adoption; broad uptake likely awaits regulatory action and maturing survival data.
