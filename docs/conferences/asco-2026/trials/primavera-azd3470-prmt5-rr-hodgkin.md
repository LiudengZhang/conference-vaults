# PRIMAVERA — AZD3470 (PRMT5 inhibitor) monotherapy, relapsed/refractory classic Hodgkin lymphoma, ≥3rd line

> **Abstract:** 7003 · **Session:** Hematologic Malignancies—Lymphoma and CLL, Sat May 30, 2026, 4:00–4:12 PM CT · **Presenter:** Enrico Derenzini, MD, PhD (Istituto Europeo di Oncologia / University of Milan, Milan, Italy) · **Embargo:** per ASCO Meeting Library

## At a glance
- **Sponsor:** AstraZeneca
- **PI / presenter:** Enrico Derenzini, MD, PhD (Istituto Europeo di Oncologia / University of Milan)
- **NCT ID:** NCT06137144
- **Phase:** Phase I/II (modular; this readout = Phase I dose-escalation)
- **Design:** Open-label, modular dose-escalation/expansion; ascending once-daily oral AZD3470 monotherapy across dose levels
- **N:** 59 evaluable for response (cHL dose-escalation cohort)
- **Primary endpoint(s):** Safety/tolerability (DLTs, MTD/RP2D); efficacy by ORR (investigator/IRC)
- **Comparator:** None (single-arm monotherapy)
- **Indication:** Relapsed/refractory classic Hodgkin lymphoma (R/R cHL)
- **Line:** Heavily pretreated — median 6 prior lines (range 3–15); all post-brentuximab vedotin and post-anti–PD-1

## Headline result
In the first safety/efficacy readout of the Phase I PRIMAVERA dose-escalation cohort, oral PRMT5 inhibitor AZD3470 monotherapy produced an overall ORR of 44% (26/59 evaluable) in heavily pretreated R/R cHL. Responses were strongly dose-dependent: no responses at ≤100 mg, vs 53% ORR / 27% CR at 450 mg daily (n=15) and 63% ORR / 44% CR at 600 mg daily (n=16); pooled ≥450 mg gave 58% ORR / 35% CR. Pre-meeting trade-press previews highlighted a peak of *up to 80% ORR with 50% CR at the optimal dose level* among responders. The population was high-risk (median 6 prior lines, 21% with ≥10 prior lines, 68% Ann Arbor stage IV); all had failed brentuximab vedotin and anti–PD-1. No dose-limiting toxicities, treatment-related discontinuations, or treatment-related deaths were reported; any-grade AEs occurred in ~91% (grade ≥3 ~40%), most commonly anemia (27%), fatigue (21%), decreased appetite (15%) and dysgeusia (15%). Median exposure 12.9 weeks; 51% remained on treatment at cutoff. No HR or p-value (single-arm).

## Mechanism / class
AZD3470 is a selective, oral protein arginine methyltransferase 5 (PRMT5) inhibitor. PRMT5 is a synthetic-lethal vulnerability when MTAP (methylthioadenosine phosphorylase) is epigenetically silenced — reported in >80% of cHL — making PRMT5 inhibition mechanistically rational in this disease. No PRMT5 inhibitor is approved; AZD3470 is investigational, first-in-human in the PRIMAVERA program.

## Discussant takeaway
Framing reported in trade-press preview coverage (Matusar, via CancerNetwork) positioned the data as addressing "a need for patients with relapsed/refractory classic Hodgkin lymphoma who progressed following checkpoint inhibition and brentuximab vedotin" — a setting with few effective options. Formal ASCO discussant remarks not separately reported.

## Practice-changing?
Pending. This is early Phase I monotherapy data: encouraging, dose-dependent single-agent activity (CR up to ~44% at 600 mg) with a clean safety profile in a population that has exhausted brentuximab vedotin and PD-1 blockade. It supports advancing AZD3470 (RP2D selection, expansion/combinations) but is not yet practice-changing without larger, confirmatory data and durability/PFS readouts.
