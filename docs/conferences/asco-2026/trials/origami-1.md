# OrigAMI-1 — Amivantamab (± FOLFOX/FOLFIRI), RAS/BRAF wild-type metastatic colorectal cancer, chemorefractory & 1L

> **Abstract:** 3548 (poster) · **Session:** Gastrointestinal Cancer — Colorectal and Anal poster session, ASCO Annual Meeting (May 29 – Jun 2, 2026, Chicago) · **Presenter:** Janssen/J&J OrigAMI-1 investigators · **Embargo:** per ASCO

## At a glance
- **Sponsor:** Janssen Research & Development / Johnson & Johnson
- **PI / presenter:** OrigAMI-1 study investigators (Janssen/J&J)
- **NCT ID:** NCT05379595
- **Phase:** 1b/2
- **Design:** Open-label, multicenter, multi-cohort. Monotherapy cohorts (left-sided anti-EGFR–naïve, left-sided anti-EGFR–pretreated, right-sided) plus combination cohorts of amivantamab + FOLFOX or FOLFIRI. Enriched for KRAS/NRAS/BRAF and EGFR-ectodomain wild-type, no ERBB2/HER2 amplification.
- **N:** 94 (monotherapy, chemorefractory cohort, JCO publication); combination cohorts reported separately
- **Primary endpoint(s):** Objective response rate (ORR) by RECIST; safety. Secondary: duration of response (DOR), PFS, biomarker correlates.
- **Comparator:** None (single-arm, phase 1b/2)
- **Indication:** RAS/BRAF wild-type metastatic colorectal cancer (mCRC)
- **Line:** Chemorefractory (monotherapy, median 2 prior lines) and first-/second-line (combination cohorts)

## Headline result
The OrigAMI-1 data presented at the **2026 ASCO Annual Meeting (abstract 3548)** is a **biomarker analysis across tumor subtypes** in RAS/BRAF wild-type mCRC, supporting the EGFR–MET mechanism of amivantamab. Reported translational findings: amivantamab treatment downregulated EGFR and cell-cycle pathway signatures and upregulated immune signatures — significant increases in dendritic-cell signature (*P*<.005) and T-cell–inflamed signature (*P*<.05) in paired baseline vs C3D1 tumor samples, plus increased cytolytic/NK-mediated cytotoxicity scores; higher baseline EGFR-ligand (AREG/EREG) expression correlated with benefit (in cohort A, AREG-high vs -low PFS 9.1 vs 4.5 months, *P*=.0076). The underlying efficacy (reported in the JCO publication and at ASCO GI 2026, not the Annual Meeting): monotherapy ORR was **29%** (left-sided, anti-EGFR–naïve), **19%** (left-sided, anti-EGFR–pretreated), and **22%** (right-sided) across N=94 chemorefractory patients (median 2 prior lines). In the chemotherapy-combination cohorts, *first-line ORR was ~73% with durable responses beyond 16 months, and ~49% ORR overall per J&J topline (ASCO GI 2026, abstract 166)*.

## Mechanism / class
Amivantamab (RYBREVANT) is a bispecific antibody targeting EGFR and MET with immune-cell–directing (Fc) activity. FDA-approved across EGFR-mutant NSCLC settings (exon 20 insertions; in combination with chemotherapy and with lazertinib). In mCRC it is investigational; OrigAMI-1 is the proof-of-concept phase 1b/2, with phase 3 confirmatory studies ongoing (OrigAMI-2 in 1L left-sided RAS/BRAF WT, OrigAMI-3 with FOLFIRI in previously treated disease).

## Practice-changing?
Pending. OrigAMI-1 is a single-arm phase 1b/2 generating hypothesis-level efficacy and supportive translational/biomarker data; the ASCO 2026 Annual Meeting contribution (abstract 3548) is a biomarker poster, not a randomized efficacy readout. The durable first-line combination responses and biomarker rationale support the ongoing randomized phase 3 OrigAMI-2/-3 trials, which will determine whether amivantamab displaces first-generation anti-EGFR antibodies (cetuximab) in RAS/BRAF wild-type mCRC.
