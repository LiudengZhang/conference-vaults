# OptimUM-02 — Darovasertib + crizotinib, 1L HLA-A*02:01-negative metastatic uveal melanoma

> **Abstract:** LBA9503 · **Session:** Melanoma/Skin Cancers — Oral Abstract Session, Mon Jun 1, 2026, 8:00–11:00 AM CT · **Presenter:** Marlana M. Orloff, MD (Thomas Jefferson University Hospital, Philadelphia) · **Embargo:** at presentation

## At a glance
- **Sponsor:** IDEAYA Biosciences (in collaboration with Servier)
- **PI / presenter:** Marlana M. Orloff, MD — Thomas Jefferson University Hospital
- **NCT ID:** NCT05987332
- **Phase:** 2/3 (multiarm, multistage), randomized, open-label, registrational
- **Design:** Darovasertib 300 mg BID + crizotinib 200 mg BID vs investigator's choice of therapy (ICT); randomized 2:1
- **N:** 313 randomized (210 darovasertib + crizotinib; 103 ICT)
- **Primary endpoint(s):** PFS by BICR
- **Comparator:** ICT — ipilimumab + nivolumab (~76–77%) or pembrolizumab (~24%)
- **Indication:** First-line HLA-A*02:01-negative metastatic uveal melanoma
- **Line:** 1L

## Headline result
The darovasertib + crizotinib combination met the primary endpoint with a statistically significant improvement in median PFS by BICR: **6.9 vs 3.1 months** vs ICT (HR 0.42; 95% CI 0.30–0.59; p<0.0001), a 58% reduction in risk of progression (64% by investigator assessment). Secondary endpoint ORR by BICR was **37.1% vs 5.8%** (p<0.0001), with 5 complete responses in the combination arm and none with ICT; DCR was 73.3% vs 31.1% and median DOR 6.8 months (95% CI 5.5–11.3). OS was immature at the January 23, 2026 cutoff with an early trend favoring the combination. Grade 3/4 TRAEs were 40.6% vs 37.0%; treatment-related SAEs 9.2% vs 25.0%.

## Mechanism / class
Darovasertib is an oral, small-molecule protein kinase C (PKC) inhibitor targeting the Gq/11–PKC signaling axis activated by GNAQ/GNA11 mutations that drive nearly all uveal melanomas. Crizotinib is an oral c-MET/ALK/ROS1 inhibitor used to exploit synthetic-lethal MET dependence in combination. Neither agent is approved in uveal melanoma; the combination is under FDA RTOR review with an NDA filing planned 2H 2026 for accelerated approval. HLA-A*02:01-positive patients are eligible for tebentafusp (the only approved 1L systemic agent), so OptimUM-02 targets the HLA-A2-negative population that lacks an approved option.

## Practice-changing?
Pending → likely yes if approved. Metastatic uveal melanoma has no approved systemic therapy for HLA-A*02:01-negative patients (tebentafusp is restricted to HLA-A2-positive disease). A positive randomized phase 2/3 with a doubling of PFS, a 6-fold higher ORR, and a favorable Grade 3/4 safety profile versus checkpoint-based ICT would establish the first targeted standard of care in this orphan setting. Practice impact hinges on FDA action; OS maturation will be watched.
