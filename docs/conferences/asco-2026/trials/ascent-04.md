# ASCENT-04 / KEYNOTE-D19 — Sacituzumab govitecan + pembrolizumab, 1L PD-L1+ metastatic TNBC

> **Abstract:** LBA1000 (PFS2 / subsequent therapy) · 1013 (biomarker subgroups) · **Session:** Breast Cancer—Metastatic, oral abstract sessions, ASCO 2026 (abstract 1013 Rapid Oral, Sun May 31, 12:30 PM CT; LBA1000 embargo lifts Tue Jun 2, 7:00 AM CT) · **Presenters:** Kevin Kalinsky, MD, MS (Winship/Emory) — LBA1000; Sara M. Tolaney, MD, MPH (Dana-Farber/Harvard) — 1013

## At a glance

- **Sponsor:** Gilead Sciences (Trodelvy) in collaboration with Merck (KEYTRUDA; KEYNOTE-D19)
- **PI / presenter:** Kevin Kalinsky (LBA1000); Sara M. Tolaney (1013 biomarker analysis)
- **NCT ID:** NCT05382286
- **Phase:** 3, randomized, open-label
- **Design:** Sacituzumab govitecan + pembrolizumab vs investigator's-choice chemotherapy + pembrolizumab; abstract 1013 = prespecified/exploratory PFS by biomarker subgroup (Trop-2 H-score quartile, tBRCA status, HER2 IHC status)
- **N:** 443 (SG+pembro 221; chemo+pembro 222); Trop-2 biomarker analysis set (BAS) subset for subgroup work
- **Primary endpoint(s):** PFS by BICR (primary, positive at ASCO 2025 / *NEJM* Jan 2026); ASCO 2026 = PFS2 / subsequent-therapy update (LBA1000) and biomarker subgroup consistency (1013)
- **Comparator:** chemotherapy (investigator's choice) + pembrolizumab — active SOC, not placebo
- **Indication:** previously untreated locally advanced unresectable or metastatic TNBC, PD-L1+ (CPS ≥10)
- **Line:** first-line

## Headline result

Primary PFS (presented ASCO 2025, *NEJM* Jan 2026) showed **median PFS 11.2 vs 7.8 months** for SG+pembro vs chemo+pembro (**HR 0.65; 95% CI 0.51–0.84; P<0.001**), a 35% reduction in progression/death. At **ASCO 2026**, two follow-on analyses are presented: **LBA1000** (Kalinsky) reports PFS2 and outcomes on subsequent lines of therapy, addressing whether the first-line benefit is preserved through the next treatment line; **abstract 1013** (Tolaney) shows the PFS benefit is **consistent across all biomarker subgroups examined**, with no subgroup losing the benefit.

In the biomarker analysis, ITT median PFS was 11.2 vs 7.8 months (HR 0.65; 95% CI 0.51–0.84), and within the Trop-2 biomarker analysis set median PFS was 11.7 vs 7.8 months (HR 0.63; 95% CI 0.48–0.82). By **Trop-2 H-score quartile**, benefit deepened with higher expression but persisted even at low expression: Q1 9.3 vs 9.0 mo (HR 0.81), Q2 9.6 vs 7.4 mo (HR 0.73), Q3 13.5 vs 8.4 mo (HR 0.46; 95% CI 0.27–0.80), Q4 16.6 vs 9.2 mo (HR 0.57; 95% CI 0.33–0.99). By **tBRCA status**: wild-type 9.6 vs 7.4 mo (HR 0.67; 0.49–0.91), mutated 16.6 vs 12.9 mo (HR 0.88; 0.45–1.74), overall tBRCA-tested 11.1 vs 7.8 mo (HR 0.69; 0.52–0.91). By **HER2 IHC status**: IHC 0 16.6 vs 9.0 mo (HR 0.69; 0.46–1.04), HER2-low 11.2 vs 7.7 mo (HR 0.67; 0.48–0.92). Tolaney concluded median PFS was longer with the SG combination across subgroups, supporting broad applicability of the regimen rather than a narrow Trop-2-high-only effect. (Subgroup analyses were not powered for formal significance; p-values not reported.)

## Mechanism / class

Sacituzumab govitecan (Trodelvy) is a **Trop-2–directed antibody–drug conjugate** delivering the SN-38 (topoisomerase-I inhibitor) payload via a hydrolyzable linker; already approved in pretreated metastatic TNBC (ASCENT) and HR+/HER2− metastatic breast cancer (TROPiCS-02). Pembrolizumab is an anti–PD-1 immune checkpoint inhibitor, the established SOC partner with chemotherapy in 1L PD-L1+ (CPS ≥10) metastatic TNBC (KEYNOTE-355). ASCENT-04/KEYNOTE-D19 substitutes SG for chemotherapy alongside checkpoint blockade in the first-line setting.

## Discussant takeaway

The 2026 discussion centers on durability of benefit beyond first line: a positive PFS2 (LBA1000) would counter the concern that front-loading SG simply borrows efficacy from later lines, while the biomarker work (1013) argues against restricting SG by Trop-2/HER2/BRCA status. Trade-press framing (OncLive, CancerNetwork) positions the parent ASCENT-04 result as a potential new 1L standard of care, with the biomarker analysis reinforcing that benefit is not confined to Trop-2-high tumors. OS remained immature at the primary readout and is the outstanding confirmatory endpoint.

## Practice-changing?

**Yes (within PD-L1+ 1L mTNBC), pending mature OS.** The primary PFS win already positions SG+pembro as a potential new first-line standard for CPS ≥10 disease; the ASCO 2026 PFS2 and biomarker data reinforce breadth and durability of benefit. The subgroup analysis removes a key barrier to adoption by showing benefit retained across Trop-2 quartiles, BRCA status, and HER2 IHC status, arguing against the need for Trop-2 biomarker selection to deploy the regimen. Confirmatory OS and regulatory action are the remaining gates.
