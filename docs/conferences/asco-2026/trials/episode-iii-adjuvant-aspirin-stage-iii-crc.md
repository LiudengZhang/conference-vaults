# EPISODE-III — Low-dose aspirin (100 mg/day, 3 yr) added to adjuvant chemotherapy, resected stage III colorectal cancer

> **Abstract:** LBA3508 · **Session:** GI Cancer — Colorectal and Anal (Oral Abstract Session) · **Presenter:** Atsuo Takashima, MD, PhD (National Cancer Center Hospital, Tokyo)

## At a glance
- **Sponsor:** Japan Clinical Oncology Group (JCOG)
- **PI / presenter:** Atsuo Takashima, MD, PhD — National Cancer Center Hospital, Tokyo
- **NCT ID:** jRCTs031180009 (Japan registry; no NCT listed)
- **Phase:** 3
- **Design:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled; aspirin 100 mg/day vs placebo for 3 years on top of standard adjuvant chemotherapy (mFOLFOX6, CAPOX, or capecitabine)
- **N:** 882 enrolled (876 in eligible-patient analysis population)
- **Primary endpoint(s):** Disease-free survival (DFS)
- **Comparator:** Placebo
- **Indication:** Resected stage III colorectal cancer after curative resection
- **Line:** Adjuvant

## Headline result
Three years of adjuvant low-dose aspirin (100 mg/day) did **not** significantly improve DFS over placebo in resected stage III colorectal cancer. The 3-year DFS rate was 78.8% with aspirin vs 75.4% with placebo (HR 0.84, 95% CI 0.65–1.09; one-sided P = 0.099), missing the prespecified significance boundary despite a numerical 3.4-point absolute benefit. DFS events: 107 (aspirin) vs 124 (placebo). Relapse-free survival trended similarly (HR 0.87). Overall survival was immature and directionally null (HR 1.02, 95% CI 0.65–1.60), with the final OS analysis planned for 2028. Grade ≥3 aspirin-related adverse events occurred in <1% of patients.

## Mechanism / class
Aspirin is a non-selective COX-1/COX-2 inhibitor. Its proposed anti-colorectal-cancer activity is linked to suppression of prostaglandin/PGE2-driven tumor signaling and, in particular, to PI3K/PIK3CA-pathway–altered tumors (the rationale behind the broader adjuvant-aspirin program). No oncology approval; widely used as antiplatelet/anti-inflammatory.

## Discussant takeaway
Not reported in available sources.

## Practice-changing?
**No.** The primary DFS endpoint was not met in an unselected stage III population, and OS is immature. Exploratory biomarker analyses (PI3K/PIK3CA) are ongoing and slated for a collaborative meta-analysis; any future role for adjuvant aspirin would likely hinge on a molecularly selected subgroup rather than all-comers.
