# DESTINY-Breast09 — Trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd) + pertuzumab, 1L HER2+ advanced/metastatic breast cancer (response-durability exploratory analysis)

> **Abstract:** 1021 · **Session:** Breast Cancer—Metastatic, 2026 ASCO Annual Meeting · **Presenter:** Yeon Hee Park, MD, PhD (Samsung Medical Center, Seoul) · **Embargo:** per ASCO meeting embargo

## At a glance
- **Sponsor:** AstraZeneca / Daiichi Sankyo
- **PI / presenter:** Yeon Hee Park, MD, PhD (Samsung Medical Center)
- **NCT ID:** NCT04784715
- **Phase:** 3 (this report = exploratory post hoc analysis by best confirmed response)
- **Design:** Randomized, open-label, 3-arm; this analysis pools the T-DXd + pertuzumab arm and stratifies outcomes by best confirmed response (CR vs PR vs SD/PD)
- **N:** ~1,157 randomized overall (T-DXd + pertuzumab vs T-DXd monotherapy vs THP); analysis within the T-DXd + pertuzumab arm
- **Primary endpoint(s):** PFS by blinded independent central review (parent trial); this analysis reports duration of response (DOR), PFS, treatment duration, and safety by response subgroup
- **Comparator:** Taxane + trastuzumab + pertuzumab (THP) — parent-trial control
- **Indication:** HER2-positive locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer
- **Line:** First-line (1L)

## Headline result
In this ASCO 2026 exploratory analysis of the T-DXd + pertuzumab arm, durable complete and partial responses translated into markedly prolonged disease control. Median treatment duration was **28.0 months in patients achieving CR** and **22.2 months in those achieving PR**, versus **4.4 months in patients with SD/PD**. Median duration of response was **39.2 months (CR) vs 26.4 months (PR)**. The parent DESTINY-Breast09 trial (ASCO 2025 plenary; NEJM) established the regimen's benefit: median PFS **40.7 months** with T-DXd + pertuzumab vs **26.9 months** with THP, HR **0.56** (44% reduction in risk of progression or death). The analysis supports that achieving CR/PR with T-DXd + pertuzumab is associated with extended treatment benefit in the 1L setting.

## Mechanism / class
T-DXd (Enhertu) is a HER2-directed antibody–drug conjugate pairing trastuzumab with a topoisomerase I inhibitor payload (deruxtecan) via a cleavable linker, with bystander effect. Pertuzumab is a HER2 dimerization-inhibiting monoclonal antibody. T-DXd + pertuzumab received US approval in December 2025 for 1L HER2-positive advanced/metastatic breast cancer on the strength of DESTINY-Breast09.

## Practice-changing?
Pending/confirmatory. The parent DESTINY-Breast09 result is already practice-changing and underpinned the December 2025 US approval, establishing T-DXd + pertuzumab as a new 1L benchmark. This exploratory response-durability analysis is supportive/descriptive — it reinforces the durability narrative and informs prognostic discussion but does not itself change practice.

## Safety
Safety findings were consistent with the known profiles of T-DXd and pertuzumab. Rates of interstitial lung disease (ILD) / pneumonitis were similar across the CR, PR, and SD/PD response groups.
