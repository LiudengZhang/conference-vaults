# ANVIL (ALCHEMIST EA5142) — Adjuvant nivolumab after surgery + chemo, resected EGFR/ALK-wild-type NSCLC, adjuvant

> **Abstract:** 8000 · **Session:** Lung Cancer — Non-Small Cell oral abstract session, ASCO Annual Meeting (May 29 – Jun 2, 2026, Chicago) · **Presenter:** Jamie E. Chaft, MD (Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center / ECOG-ACRIN) · **Embargo:** per ASCO; results simultaneously published in JAMA

## At a glance
- **Sponsor:** ECOG-ACRIN Cancer Research Group / National Cancer Institute (NCI); nivolumab supplied by Bristol Myers Squibb
- **PI / presenter:** Jamie E. Chaft, MD (Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center)
- **NCT ID:** NCT02595944
- **Phase:** 3
- **Design:** Randomized, open-label; nivolumab vs observation/best supportive care after complete surgical resection and standard adjuvant chemotherapy (and radiation when indicated). Part of the ALCHEMIST platform.
- **N:** 935 (nivolumab n=466; observation n=469)
- **Primary endpoint(s):** Disease-free survival (DFS) in the ITT and PD-L1–high (TPS ≥50%) populations
- **Comparator:** Observation / best supportive care
- **Indication:** Resected stage IB–IIIA NSCLC, EGFR- and ALK-wild-type
- **Line:** Adjuvant (post-surgery, post-chemotherapy)

## Headline result
Adjuvant nivolumab did **not** improve disease-free survival versus observation. In the ITT population, median DFS was 71.3 months with nivolumab vs 68.8 months with observation (HR 0.97; 95% CI 0.81–1.17; P=.39). The PD-L1–high (TPS ≥50%) subgroup also showed no significant benefit: median DFS 89.9 vs 78.5 months (HR 0.86; 95% CI 0.59–1.25; P=.43). Because the primary DFS endpoint was not met, overall survival was not formally tested under the hierarchical plan; exploratory OS was numerically neutral in ITT (HR 1.02; 95% CI 0.82–1.26) and the PD-L1–high subset (HR 0.82; 95% CI 0.53–1.28). Median follow-up was ~72.6 months. This is the largest US cooperative-group trial of adjuvant immunotherapy in NSCLC, and the result was negative.

## Mechanism / class
Nivolumab is an anti–PD-1 monoclonal antibody (immune checkpoint inhibitor). It is approved across multiple NSCLC settings, including perioperative/neoadjuvant use (e.g., neoadjuvant nivolumab + chemotherapy, CheckMate 816). EA5142/ANVIL tested the purely adjuvant, post-chemotherapy monotherapy strategy.

## Practice-changing?
No. The trial is negative for its primary DFS endpoint in both the ITT and PD-L1–high populations, and exploratory OS showed no benefit. The result argues against adjuvant single-agent nivolumab administered after completion of standard adjuvant chemotherapy, and contrasts with positive perioperative/neoadjuvant chemo-immunotherapy regimens and with adjuvant atezolizumab (IMpower010) and pembrolizumab (PEARLS/KEYNOTE-091) data, reinforcing questions about sequencing and the value of adjuvant-only checkpoint monotherapy.
