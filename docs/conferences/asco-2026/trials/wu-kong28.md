# WU-KONG28 — Sunvozertinib monotherapy vs platinum-doublet chemotherapy, 1L advanced EGFR exon20ins NSCLC

> **Abstract:** LBA8500 · **Session:** Lung Cancer—Non-Small Cell Metastatic, Oral Abstract Session, Fri May 29 2026, 1:00–1:12 PM CT, Hall D2 · **Presenter:** John V. Heymach, MD, PhD (MD Anderson Cancer Center) · **Embargo:** May 29 2026

## At a glance
- **Sponsor:** Dizal Pharmaceutical (Shanghai)
- **PI / presenter:** John V. Heymach, MD, PhD — Chair, Thoracic/Head and Neck Medical Oncology, MD Anderson Cancer Center
- **NCT ID:** NCT05668988
- **Phase:** 3 (multinational, randomized, confirmatory; 15 countries across Asia, Europe, North America, South America)
- **Design:** Open-label, randomized 1:1 to sunvozertinib 300 mg PO once daily vs investigator-choice platinum-based doublet (carboplatin + pemetrexed ± pemetrexed maintenance)
- **N:** 324 (1:1 randomization)
- **Primary endpoint(s):** PFS by blinded independent central review (BICR) per RECIST 1.1
- **Comparator:** Platinum-doublet chemotherapy (carboplatin/pemetrexed)
- **Indication:** Treatment-naïve locally advanced or metastatic non-squamous NSCLC with EGFR exon 20 insertion mutation (exon20ins)
- **Line:** First-line

## Headline result
First-line sunvozertinib monotherapy significantly prolonged BICR-assessed PFS vs platinum-doublet chemotherapy, meeting the primary endpoint: median PFS 10.3 months (95% CI, 8.3–14.0) vs 7.5 months (95% CI, 6.7–8.5), HR 0.65 (95% CI, 0.50–0.85; P = .0008) — a 35% reduction in risk of progression or death. Confirmed ORR by BICR was 58.9% (95% CI, 50.9–66.5) with sunvozertinib vs 31.1% (95% CI, 24.0–38.8) with chemotherapy. Adverse reactions were described as generally manageable and reversible, with no new safety signals. Results were published simultaneously in the *New England Journal of Medicine*.

## Mechanism / class
Sunvozertinib (DZD9008, ZEGFROVY) is an oral, irreversible, EGFR exon 20 insertion–selective tyrosine kinase inhibitor that also covers other uncommon EGFR mutations while sparing wild-type EGFR. It received accelerated/conditional approval (China NMPA 2023; US FDA accelerated approval July 2025) for EGFR exon20ins NSCLC after platinum-based chemotherapy; WU-KONG28 is the confirmatory first-line registrational study.

## Discussant takeaway
Presenter framing (Heymach): the trial "marks a clinical turning point … offering a practice-changing alternative that may spare treatment-naïve patients from initial cytotoxic treatment." Independent discussant commentary not separately captured in available reporting.

## Practice-changing?
Likely yes for 1L EGFR exon20ins NSCLC. This is the first positive randomized phase 3 of a targeted oral agent vs chemotherapy in the front-line exon20ins setting, with a statistically significant PFS benefit, roughly doubled ORR, and simultaneous NEJM publication — positioning sunvozertinib to move from post-chemo (current approved setting) into first line. OS data remain immature.
