# HARMONi-6 — Ivonescimab + chemo vs tislelizumab + chemo, 1L squamous NSCLC

> **Abstract:** LBA4 · **Session:** Plenary, Sun May 31, 2026, Hall B1 · **Embargo:** lifted 7:00 AM CT May 31

## At a glance

- **Sponsor:** Akeso (partner: Summit Therapeutics)
- **Phase:** 3 (China)
- **Design:** Randomized; ivonescimab + platinum chemotherapy vs tislelizumab + platinum chemotherapy
- **Primary endpoint(s):** OS (and PFS) — **OS positive**
- **Comparator:** tislelizumab (anti–PD-1) + chemo — an active IO comparator, not placebo
- **Indication:** previously untreated advanced squamous NSCLC
- **Line:** first-line

## Headline result

Ivonescimab + chemo delivered **superior overall survival vs an active PD-1 + chemo comparator: median OS 27.89 vs 23.69 months.** PFS was previously positive (ESMO 2025); the OS readout was the load-bearing watch-item of the meeting for this class and was selected for the Plenary. Beating an anti–PD-1 arm head-to-head on OS is the key result.

## Mechanism / class

Ivonescimab is a **PD-1 × VEGF bispecific antibody**. HARMONi-6 is the most mature randomized OS evidence for the PD-1×VEGF class and is read as class-defining — though the comparator and population are China-based, framing the global-generalizability debate.

## Discussant takeaway

Discussion centered on (1) generalizability of a China-only trial with a tislelizumab backbone to non-China practice, and (2) PD-1×VEGF as a platform class — read-through to AbbVie RC148, BMS pumitamig, and the broader bispecific wave.

## Practice-changing?

**Class-defining if it generalizes** — first OS win for PD-1×VEGF over single-agent checkpoint + chemo; global registrational strategy (Summit) is the gating question for non-China practice.
