# EPCORE DLBCL-1 — Epcoritamab (CD3xCD20 bispecific) monotherapy vs investigator's choice chemoimmunotherapy, relapsed/refractory DLBCL (2L+)

> **Abstract:** TBD · **Session:** Oral Abstract Session — Hematologic Malignancies: Lymphoma and Chronic Lymphocytic Leukemia (first full-results presentation), ASCO 2026 · **Presenter:** Not yet captured in available reporting · **Embargo:** May 2026

## At a glance
- **Sponsor:** Genmab (co-developed with AbbVie)
- **PI / presenter:** Not captured in available reporting
- **NCT ID:** NCT04628494
- **Phase:** 3
- **Design:** Global, open-label, multicenter, randomized — epcoritamab monotherapy vs investigator's choice chemoimmunotherapy (R-GemOx or bendamustine + rituximab [BR])
- **N:** 483 patients
- **Primary endpoint(s):** Progression-free survival (PFS)
- **Comparator:** Investigator's choice chemoimmunotherapy (R-GemOx or BR)
- **Indication:** Relapsed/refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL), ineligible for high-dose chemotherapy + autologous stem cell transplant
- **Line:** Second-line and beyond (≥1 prior line; 73% had ≥2 prior lines)

## Headline result
EPCORE DLBCL-1 is the first phase 3 study of a CD3xCD20 T-cell–engaging bispecific **monotherapy** to improve PFS over chemoimmunotherapy in R/R DLBCL. Epcoritamab improved PFS versus investigator's choice chemotherapy (*HR 0.74; 95% CI, 0.60–0.92 — per Genmab/AbbVie topline, Jan 16, 2026*). Improvements were also reported in complete response rate, duration of response, and time to next treatment. Overall survival favored epcoritamab numerically but was not statistically significant (*HR 0.96; 95% CI, 0.77–1.20 — per topline, Jan 16, 2026*). The global trial randomized 483 patients ineligible for high-dose chemotherapy and autologous stem cell transplant, 73% of whom had received ≥2 prior lines. (Full presented per-arm medians, CR rates, ORR, and DoR not yet captured from indexed ASCO 2026 reporting.)

## Mechanism / class
Epcoritamab (Epkinly/Tepkinly; DuoBody CD3xCD20) is a subcutaneous bispecific antibody that engages CD3 on T cells and CD20 on malignant B cells to drive T-cell–mediated killing. It is already FDA-approved as monotherapy for R/R DLBCL after ≥2 prior lines (accelerated approval based on EPCORE NHL-1). EPCORE DLBCL-1 is the confirmatory randomized phase 3 intended to convert that accelerated approval and broaden use into earlier R/R lines.

## Practice-changing?
Pending. As the first positive randomized phase 3 of a bispecific monotherapy beating chemoimmunotherapy on PFS in R/R DLBCL, it strengthens the case for off-the-shelf epcoritamab in transplant-ineligible patients and supports conversion of the accelerated approval. However, the lack of an OS benefit (HR 0.96) tempers the signal and will be central to discussant and regulatory framing; adoption likely awaits the full presented dataset and regulatory action.
