# CT041-CG4006 (Cohort 3) — Satri-cel (CT041), Claudin18.2 CAR T, advanced gastric/GEJ adenocarcinoma, sequential therapy after 1L

> **Abstract:** 2557 · **Session:** Poster — May 30, 2026, 13:30–16:30 CDT · **Presenter:** CARsgen Therapeutics (presenter not individually named in release) · **Embargo:** May 30, 2026

## At a glance
- **Sponsor:** CARsgen Therapeutics Holdings Limited (investigator-initiated trial)
- **PI / presenter:** Not individually named in company release
- **NCT ID:** NCT03874897
- **Phase:** Phase 1 (open-label, multi-cohort)
- **Design:** Subgroup analysis of Cohort 3 — satri-cel given sequentially after first-line therapy in Claudin18.2+ gastric/GEJ adenocarcinoma; single infusion at 2.50×10⁸ cells
- **N:** 5 (Claudin18.2+ gastric/GEJ; poor-prognosis — diffuse/signet-ring, peritoneal mets)
- **Primary endpoint(s):** Safety and efficacy (ORR, DOR, PFS)
- **Comparator:** None (single-arm)
- **Indication:** Advanced gastric / gastro-esophageal junction adenocarcinoma
- **Line:** Sequential / maintenance after first-line therapy

## Headline result
In this long-term (>4.5 year) follow-up of Cohort 3 from CT041-CG4006 (NCT03874897), 5 Claudin18.2+ gastric/GEJ patients received satri-cel sequentially after first-line therapy. All 4 patients with measurable target lesions achieved a confirmed objective response (ORR 100%, 4/4). Median duration of response was not reached and median PFS measured from initiation of first-line therapy was 20.9 months, at the October 18, 2025 data cutoff (median follow-up 54.6 months from initial 1L therapy). Two patients achieving clinical benefit underwent surgical resection after satri-cel and both remained alive at 58.1 and 51.1 months. No grade ≥3 CRS, ICANS, treatment-related deaths, grade ≥3 infections, or febrile neutropenia were reported. *All figures per CARsgen press release, May 30, 2026; small N=5 subgroup.*

## Mechanism / class
Satricabtagene autoleucel (satri-cel, CT041) is an autologous CAR T-cell therapy targeting Claudin18.2, a tight-junction protein highly expressed in gastric/GEJ and pancreatic adenocarcinoma. In the randomized phase 2 CT041-ST-01 trial (later-line G/GEJ), satri-cel improved PFS vs physician's choice (published in The Lancet, 2025). In China, the NMPA accepted the satri-cel NDA (June 2025) with Breakthrough Therapy Designation (March 2025) and Priority Review (May 2025) for Claudin18.2+ advanced G/GEJA after ≥2 prior lines.

## Practice-changing?
No / pending. This is a hypothesis-generating N=5 subgroup from an early-phase IIT, not a controlled comparison. The durable responses and 20.9-month PFS-from-1L in a poor-prognosis population (diffuse/signet-ring, peritoneal mets) support moving satri-cel earlier into a sequential/maintenance setting after 1L, but practice change awaits a randomized first-line-adjacent study. Regulatory momentum for satri-cel rests on the later-line CT041-ST-01 data, not this cohort.
