# HORIZON-CRC01 — Trastuzumab rezetecan (HER2 ADC), HER2+ RAS/RAF wild-type chemo-refractory advanced colorectal cancer, 3rd line

> **Abstract:** 3505 · **Session:** Gastrointestinal Cancer—Colorectal and Anal · **Presenter:** Jin Li, MD (Shanghai GoBroad Cancer Hospital Affiliated to China Pharmaceutical University, Shanghai, China) · **Embargo:** per ASCO 2026 embargo policy

## At a glance
- **Sponsor:** Jiangsu Hengrui Pharmaceuticals (Hengrui Pharma)
- **PI / presenter:** Jin Li, MD; co-lead Ying Yuan, MD (Second Affiliated Hospital, Zhejiang University School of Medicine)
- **NCT ID:** not specified in available sources (phase 1 program: NCT04513223)
- **Phase:** 3
- **Design:** Multicenter, open-label, randomized 2:1; stratified by HER2 status and performance status
- **N:** 130 (86 trastuzumab rezetecan vs 44 control)
- **Primary endpoint(s):** Progression-free survival (PFS) by independent review committee
- **Comparator:** Physician's choice — TAS-102, fruquintinib, or regorafenib
- **Indication:** HER2-positive, RAS/RAF wild-type advanced/metastatic colorectal cancer, progression after second-line therapy
- **Line:** 3rd line (chemo-refractory)

## Headline result
In HER2-positive, RAS/RAF wild-type chemo-refractory advanced CRC, trastuzumab rezetecan 4.8 mg/kg IV q3w significantly improved median PFS to 5.5 vs 2.8 months versus physician's-choice standard of care (HR 0.33, p<0.0001). Confirmed ORR was 40.7% vs 4.5% (p<0.001). Median OS was not reached with an immature OS HR of 0.77 (p=0.2651). Grade ≥3 treatment-related adverse events were comparable between arms (48.8% vs 50%); two treatment-related deaths occurred in the trastuzumab rezetecan arm.

## Mechanism / class
Trastuzumab rezetecan (SHR-A1811) is a next-generation HER2-directed antibody–drug conjugate pairing a trastuzumab-based anti-HER2 antibody with a cleavable linker and a topoisomerase I inhibitor payload. It received its first regulatory approval in China in 2025. The same agent is in development across HER2-altered tumors including HER2-mutant NSCLC (HORIZON-Lung) and HER2-expressing gastric/GEJ cancer.

## Practice-changing?
Pending. The first randomized phase 3 of a HER2 ADC in chemo-refractory HER2+ mCRC, with a striking PFS HR (0.33) and a >9-fold ORR advantage, establishes trastuzumab rezetecan as a strong candidate for this biomarker-selected niche. OS remains immature, the comparator chemotherapies are low-efficacy controls, and the trial was conducted entirely in China — global regulatory and guideline adoption will depend on OS maturity and ex-China data.
