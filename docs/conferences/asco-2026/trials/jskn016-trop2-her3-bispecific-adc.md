# JSKN016-101 — JSKN016 (TROP2/HER3 bispecific ADC), HER2-negative advanced/metastatic breast cancer, late-line

> **Abstract:** 1123 · **Session:** Breast Cancer—Metastatic, Poster Session (June 1, 2026, 1:30–4:30 PM CT) · **Presenter:** Herui Yao, Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hospital, Sun Yat-sen University · **Embargo:** Jun 1, 2026

## At a glance
- **Sponsor:** Alphamab Oncology (9966.HK)
- **PI / presenter:** Herui Yao (Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hospital, Sun Yat-sen University)
- **NCT ID:** NCT06592417
- **Phase:** Phase I (dose-escalation / dose-expansion, China)
- **Design:** First-in-human, open-label monotherapy study in advanced solid tumors; ASCO 2026 reports the HER2-negative locally advanced/metastatic breast cancer cohort (incl. mTNBC and HR+/HER2- BC)
- **N:** HER2-negative BC cohort (exact N not disclosed in pre-meeting topline)
- **Primary endpoint(s):** Safety/tolerability, MTD/RP2D; preliminary antitumor activity (ORR, DCR per RECIST v1.1)
- **Comparator:** None (single-arm)
- **Indication:** HER2-negative (incl. triple-negative) locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer
- **Line:** Heavily pretreated / late-line (multiple prior lines)

## Headline result
*Per Alphamab Oncology pre-meeting topline (Apr 23, 2026), no quantitative cohort-level efficacy was released for abstract 1123;* the company states JSKN016 showed "very promising efficacy and superior safety profile in late-line BC patients." Prior first-in-human data for the same program — presented at **ASCO 2025** (mTNBC subset) — reported a preliminary **ORR of 80% (4 PR, 1 SD among 5 efficacy-evaluable mTNBC pts)** with no cardiac toxicity, no CRS, no treatment-related ILD, and no AE-related discontinuations or deaths within the studied dose. The ASCO 2026 poster expands these observations to the broader HER2-negative cohort; presented numbers (ORR/DCR/N/safety) should be read off the abstract 1123 poster on Jun 1 rather than from press releases.

## Mechanism / class
First-in-class **TROP2/HER3 bispecific antibody-drug conjugate**. Built on Alphamab's single-domain + bispecific antibody platforms; site-specific glycosylation conjugation yields a homogeneous, stable ADC with **DAR 4** carrying a **topoisomerase I inhibitor** payload. Dual TROP2 and/or HER3 binding is intended to broaden tumor targeting and widen the therapeutic window vs single-target TROP2 ADCs. No marketed approvals; a Phase III study in TNBC is ongoing (first patient dosed reported 2025/2026).

## Practice-changing?
**Pending.** Early-phase, single-arm, China-only, small HER2-negative cohort with no quantitative ASCO 2026 topline yet public. Of interest as a first-in-class dual-target ADC in a field crowded by single-target TROP2 ADCs (sacituzumab govitecan, datopotamab deruxtecan), but Phase III randomized data are required before any practice impact.
