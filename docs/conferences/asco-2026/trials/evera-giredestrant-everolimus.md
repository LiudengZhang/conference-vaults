# evERA — Giredestrant + Everolimus, ER+/HER2− advanced breast cancer, post-CDK4/6i

> **Abstract:** 1016 · **Session:** Breast Cancer—Metastatic (poster/oral presentation) · **Presenter:** Komal Jhaveri (Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center), on behalf of evERA investigators · **Conference:** 2026 ASCO Annual Meeting, May 29 – Jun 2, Chicago

## At a glance
- **Sponsor:** Roche / Genentech
- **PI / presenter:** Komal Jhaveri (MSKCC); co-authors incl. Hope S. Rugo, Sara M. Tolaney, Miguel Martín, Erica L. Mayer
- **NCT ID:** NCT05306340
- **Phase:** 3
- **Design:** Randomized, open-label, multicenter; giredestrant + everolimus vs physician's-choice endocrine therapy (exemestane) + everolimus
- **N:** ~320 randomized
- **Primary endpoint(s):** Investigator-assessed PFS in ITT and in the ESR1-mutant population (co-primary)
- **Comparator:** Exemestane + everolimus (standard-of-care endocrine therapy + everolimus)
- **Indication:** ER+/HER2− locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer
- **Line:** Post-CDK4/6 inhibitor (prior CDK4/6i + endocrine therapy)

## Headline result
The ASCO 2026 presentation (Abstract 1016) reported post-progression analyses of evERA. PFS2 (progression-free survival on next-line therapy) favored giredestrant + everolimus over standard endocrine therapy + everolimus in the ITT population (median 19.0 vs 13.2 mo; HR 0.69, 95% CI 0.51–0.92) and in the ESR1-mutant subgroup (median 19.0 vs 12.5 mo; HR 0.61, 95% CI 0.40–0.93). Chemotherapy-free survival also favored the giredestrant arm (median 11.1 vs 7.9 mo; HR 0.61), with the largest benefit in ESR1-mutant patients (HR 0.46, 95% CI 0.32–0.66). These follow the previously reported (ESMO 2025) co-primary PFS results: ITT median 8.77 vs 5.49 mo (HR 0.56, 95% CI 0.44–0.71; P < .0001) and ESR1-mutant median 9.99 vs 5.45 mo (HR 0.38, 95% CI 0.27–0.54; P < .0001).

## Mechanism / class
Giredestrant is an oral selective estrogen receptor degrader (SERD) and full ER antagonist. Combined with the mTOR inhibitor everolimus, the regimen targets endocrine resistance — including ESR1-mutant clones that drive resistance after CDK4/6i. The FDA accepted an NDA for giredestrant + everolimus in ESR1-mutated ER+ advanced breast cancer.

## Practice-changing?
Pending. evERA is the first phase 3 to pair a next-generation oral SERD with everolimus in the post-CDK4/6i setting, and the PFS2 and chemotherapy-free-survival data strengthen the benefit signal beyond the primary PFS, particularly in ESR1-mutant disease. With an NDA under FDA review, a positive label could establish giredestrant + everolimus as a chemotherapy-sparing option after CDK4/6i, but final OS data are immature.
