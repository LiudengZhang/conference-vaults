# CHRYSALIS-2 (Cohort C) — Amivantamab + lazertinib, first-line atypical EGFR-mutated advanced NSCLC

> **Abstract:** 8501 · **Session:** Lung Cancer—Non-Small Cell Metastatic (oral), 2026 ASCO Annual Meeting, Chicago · **Presenter:** Joel W. Neal, MD, PhD (Stanford Cancer Institute) · **Embargo:** per ASCO

## At a glance
- **Sponsor:** Janssen Research & Development (Johnson & Johnson)
- **PI / presenter:** Joel W. Neal, MD, PhD — Stanford Cancer Institute, Palo Alto, CA
- **NCT ID:** NCT04077463
- **Phase:** 1/1b
- **Design:** Open-label, single-arm cohort (Cohort C of CHRYSALIS-2) — first-line amivantamab IV + oral lazertinib in treatment-naïve atypical EGFR-mutated advanced NSCLC
- **N:** 49 (treatment-naïve, atypical EGFR mutations)
- **Primary endpoint(s):** ORR (investigator-assessed); key secondary OS, PFS, DOR, safety
- **Comparator:** None (single-arm)
- **Indication:** Advanced NSCLC with atypical (non-classical) EGFR mutations — G719X, S768I, L861Q, and compound mutations
- **Line:** First-line

## Headline result
At a median follow-up of 31.3 months (data cutoff Oct 31, 2025), first-line amivantamab + lazertinib produced a **median OS of 41.0 months** (95% CI, 27.7–NE) in 49 treatment-naïve patients with atypical EGFR-mutated advanced NSCLC. Landmark OS rates were **85% at 12 months, 72% at 24 months, and 55% at 36 months**, with **46% of patients alive at 4 years**. Investigator-assessed **ORR was 57%** and **median PFS 19.5 months** (95% CI, 11.2–NE). Mutation distribution included G719X (55%), S768I/S768X (27%), and L861Q/L861X (24%), with compound mutations in 35%. The presenter reported durable responses across demographic and tumor subgroups with no new safety signals on longer follow-up. As a single-arm cohort there is no comparator HR or p-value.

## Mechanism / class
Amivantamab (Rybrevant) is a bispecific EGFR × MET antibody; lazertinib (Lazcluze) is a third-generation EGFR TKI. The combination is approved in the US/EU for first-line classical EGFR-mutant (exon 19 del / L858R) advanced NSCLC (MARIPOSA). Cohort C extends the regimen to atypical EGFR mutations, a subgroup historically associated with worse outcomes on classical EGFR TKIs than exon 19 del or L858R disease.

## Practice-changing?
Pending. Best reported survival to date in an uncommon, hard-to-treat EGFR subgroup with limited prospective data, but the evidence is a single-arm, 49-patient cohort without a randomized comparator. Supports amivantamab + lazertinib as a viable first-line option for atypical EGFR-mutant NSCLC and strengthens the rationale for a dedicated randomized/registrational study; not by itself a new standard.
