# LIBRETTO-432 — Adjuvant selpercatinib, stage IB–IIIA RET fusion+ NSCLC

> **Abstract:** LBA3 · **Session:** Plenary, Sun May 31, 2026, Hall B1 · **Embargo:** lifted 7:00 AM CT May 31

## At a glance

- **Sponsor:** Eli Lilly (Loxo@Lilly)
- **NCT ID:** NCT04819100
- **Phase:** 3
- **Design:** Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled; adjuvant selpercatinib vs placebo after definitive treatment (resection ± chemo/radiation)
- **Primary endpoint(s):** EFS (event-free survival) — **met**
- **Comparator:** placebo
- **Indication:** stage IB–IIIA RET fusion-positive NSCLC
- **Line:** adjuvant

## Headline result

Adjuvant selpercatinib **cut the risk of recurrence or death by 83% vs placebo** (EFS), a large and statistically significant benefit. No adjuvant RET-directed targeted therapy had previously been approved in this subset — LIBRETTO-432 establishes a potential new standard of care.

## Mechanism / class

Selpercatinib (Retevmo) is a selective RET kinase inhibitor. LIBRETTO-432 extends RET-TKI use from advanced disease into the curative-intent adjuvant setting, mirroring ADAURA (osimertinib, EGFR) and ALINA (alectinib, ALK).

## Discussant takeaway

The adjuvant-lung discussion focused on the familiar EFS-vs-OS and "treat-now vs at-recurrence" question for oncogene-addicted adjuvant TKIs, and on optimal duration — but the 83% EFS reduction was treated as practice-defining for RET+ resected disease.

## Practice-changing?

**Likely yes** — first adjuvant RET TKI; slots into the molecular-adjuvant paradigm alongside osimertinib and alectinib.
