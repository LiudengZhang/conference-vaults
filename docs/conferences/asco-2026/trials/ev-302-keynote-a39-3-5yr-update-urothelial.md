# EV-302 / KEYNOTE-A39 — Enfortumab vedotin + pembrolizumab, 1L locally advanced/metastatic urothelial carcinoma (3.5-year update)

> **Abstract:** 4507 · **Session:** Genitourinary Cancer — Kidney and Bladder (Oral Abstract Session), 2026 ASCO Annual Meeting, Chicago · **Presenter:** Thomas Powles, MD, PhD (Barts Cancer Centre, Queen Mary University of London) · **Embargo:** per ASCO Annual Meeting (May 29 – Jun 2, 2026)

## At a glance
- **Sponsor:** Astellas / Pfizer (Seagen) and Merck — co-development of enfortumab vedotin (Padcev) and pembrolizumab (Keytruda)
- **PI / presenter:** Thomas Powles, MD, PhD — Barts Cancer Centre, Queen Mary University of London
- **NCT ID:** NCT04223856
- **Phase:** 3
- **Design:** Open-label, randomized 1:1; EV + pembrolizumab vs platinum-based chemotherapy (gemcitabine + cisplatin or carboplatin)
- **N:** 886 (EV+P n = 442; chemo n = 444)
- **Primary endpoint(s):** Overall survival (OS) and progression-free survival (PFS)
- **Comparator:** Platinum-based chemotherapy
- **Indication:** Previously untreated locally advanced or metastatic urothelial carcinoma (la/mUC)
- **Line:** First-line (1L)

## Headline result
At a median follow-up of 42.8 months (3.5 years), EV + pembrolizumab continued to deliver a roughly doubled overall survival over chemotherapy in the ITT population: median OS 33.6 months (95% CI, 26.6–39.8) vs 15.9 months (95% CI, 13.6–18.3), with a 42-month OS rate of 44.0% vs 24.6% (HR 0.53; 95% CI, 0.45–0.63). Confirmed complete responses occurred in 30.4% of the EV+P arm (n = 133) vs 14.5% with chemotherapy (n = 64); among confirmed responders, CR rates were 45.1% vs 32.8%. CRs were durable: patients achieving CR with EV+P had a 42-month OS rate of 83.6% with median OS not yet reached, and the OS benefit held even within the CR subgroup (HR 0.36; 95% CI, 0.20–0.68). 66.2% of EV+P CRs first achieved a partial response before converting to CR.

## Mechanism / class
Enfortumab vedotin is a Nectin-4–directed antibody-drug conjugate delivering the microtubule inhibitor MMAE; pembrolizumab is an anti–PD-1 immune checkpoint inhibitor. The combination won full FDA approval in December 2023 for 1L la/mUC on the basis of the original EV-302 readout, displacing platinum chemotherapy as the standard of care regardless of cisplatin eligibility.

## Discussant takeaway
Powles framed the long-term data as confirming a "transformative" and durable survival benefit, with the responder and CR analyses underscoring that deep, durable responses translate into extended survival.

## Practice-changing?
No — confirmatory. EV + pembrolizumab is already the established 1L standard for la/mUC since the original EV-302 results; this 3.5-year update reinforces the durability of the survival advantage rather than altering practice.
