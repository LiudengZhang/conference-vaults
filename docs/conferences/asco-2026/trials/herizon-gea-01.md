# HERIZON-GEA-01 — Zanidatamab + chemotherapy ± tislelizumab vs trastuzumab + chemo, 1L HER2+ locally advanced/metastatic gastroesophageal adenocarcinoma

> **Abstract:** 4010 (PD-L1 subgroup analysis), Rapid Oral · **Session:** Gastrointestinal Cancer—Gastroesophageal, Pancreatic, and Hepatobiliary, Rapid Oral Abstract Session, Mon Jun 1 2026 · **Presenter:** Sun Young Rha, MD, PhD (Yonsei Cancer Center, Yonsei University College of Medicine, Seoul) · **Embargo:** May 27 2026 (NEJM publication + topline)

## At a glance
- **Sponsor:** BeOne Medicines (formerly BeiGene); co-developed with Jazz Pharmaceuticals (Ziihera / zanidatamab-hrii)
- **PI / presenter:** Sun Young Rha, MD, PhD (Yonsei Cancer Center, Seoul) — first author of the ASCO 2026 PD-L1 subgroup abstract
- **NCT ID:** NCT05152147
- **Phase:** 3
- **Design:** Global, randomized 1:1:1, open-label, ~300 sites across 30+ countries — (A) trastuzumab + CAPOX/FP; (B) zanidatamab + CAPOX/FP; (C) zanidatamab + tislelizumab + CAPOX/FP. Zanidatamab dosed 1800 mg (<70 kg) or 2400 mg (≥70 kg) IV Q3W; tislelizumab 200 mg IV Q3W
- **N:** 914 randomized — arm A (control) n=308; arm B (zani + chemo) n=304; arm C (zani + tisle + chemo) n=302; enrolled Dec 2021–Feb 2025
- **Primary endpoint(s):** PFS (BICR) and OS, zanidatamab arms vs trastuzumab control
- **Comparator:** Trastuzumab + chemotherapy (CAPOX or FP)
- **Indication:** Previously untreated HER2-positive locally advanced, unresectable, or metastatic gastroesophageal adenocarcinoma (stomach, GEJ, esophagus)
- **Line:** First-line (1L)

## Headline result
At a median follow-up of 26.0 months, both zanidatamab-containing regimens significantly improved both primary endpoints versus trastuzumab + chemo.

- **Triplet (arm C, zanidatamab + tislelizumab + chemo):** median OS 26.4 months (95% CI 21.5–30.3) vs 19.2 months (95% CI 16.8–21.8) for control — HR 0.72 (95% CI 0.57–0.90; P=0.0043). PFS by BICR 12.4 months vs 8.1 months (95% CI 7.0–8.9) for control — HR 0.63 (95% CI 0.51–0.78; P<0.0001), a ~37% reduction in risk of progression or death.
- **Doublet (arm B, zanidatamab + chemo):** median OS 24.4 months. PFS by BICR 12.4 months vs 8.1 months for control — HR 0.65 (95% CI 0.52–0.81; P<0.0001).
- **Duration of response:** 20.7 months (arm C), 14.3 months (arm B), 8.3 months (control).

HERIZON-GEA-01 is the first phase 3 in metastatic GEA to show median PFS >1 year and median OS >2 years.

The ASCO 2026 abstract 4010 PD-L1 subgroup analysis showed benefit was consistent across PD-L1–negative and PD-L1–positive tumors (concordant by TAP and CPS): for PD-L1 TAP <1%, 18-month PFS 50.3% and 24-month OS 63.7%; for TAP ≥1%, 18-month PFS 42.6% and 24-month OS 53.5%. Median OS reached 29.7 months in the PD-L1 TAP <1% subgroup (vs 15.8 months control) with the zanidatamab + tislelizumab + chemo triplet, and 26.4 months in TAP ≥1% (vs 21.2 months control).

Immune-mediated AEs occurred in 37.8% of triplet-arm patients (mostly grade 1–2); grade 5 drug-related AEs were 2.4% (triplet), 0.3% (zani+chemo), and 1.3% (trastuzumab). Primary results were published simultaneously in NEJM.

## Mechanism / class
Zanidatamab (Ziihera / zanidatamab-hrii) is a bispecific, biparatopic HER2-directed antibody binding two non-overlapping HER2 epitopes (ECD2 and ECD4), driving receptor clustering, internalization, and enhanced effector function. Already FDA-approved (Nov 2024) for previously treated HER2-positive (IHC 3+) biliary tract cancer. Tislelizumab (Tevimbra) is an anti–PD-1 monoclonal antibody engineered to minimize FcγR binding to macrophages.

## Discussant takeaway
The ASCO 2026 abstract is framed against the KEYNOTE-811 precedent, where the FDA narrowed pembrolizumab's HER2+ gastric indication to CPS ≥1 over durability concerns in PD-L1–low tumors. The HERIZON-GEA-01 PD-L1 subgroup data argue that the zanidatamab + tislelizumab benefit is not restricted to PD-L1–high disease, with the triplet showing among the longest OS reported in PD-L1–negative patients — a point likely to feature in indication-breadth discussion. (No named ASCO discussant reported in available sources.)

## Practice-changing?
Pending / likely yes. Both combinations are positioned by the sponsors as a new 1L standard of care, with the triplet delivering a ~7-month OS gain over trastuzumab + chemo and benefit retained across PD-L1 strata. The FDA granted priority review to the sBLA for zanidatamab + chemo ± tislelizumab in 1L HER2+ gastric/GEJ/GEA with a target action date of August 25, 2026. Primary data are published in NEJM. The ASCO 2026 subgroup analysis supports broad applicability across PD-L1 status but is confirmatory rather than itself registrational.
