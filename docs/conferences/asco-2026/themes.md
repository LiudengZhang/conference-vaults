# Themes — ASCO 2026

Cross-day synthesis of the recurring threads across the 2026 ASCO Annual Meeting (Chicago, May 29 – Jun 2). Each thread cites the specific built trial pages; LBA1–5 anchored the Sunday May 31 plenary. Cross-links reach back to [JPM 2026 themes](../jpm-2026/themes.md) and the [AACR 2026 vault](../aacr-2026/index.md) where the science rhymes.

> **Source caveat:** Several presentations (TRIPLEX, persevERA, JSKN016, revumenib maintenance, KN026-004) were embargoed or topline-only at writing — figures noted as "not disclosed" reflect that, not missing data.

---

## 1. RAS-class drugs become real

The headline of the meeting. [RASolute 302](trials/rasolute-302.md) is the **first positive phase 3 of a pan-RAS(ON) inhibitor**: 2L daraxonrasib nearly doubled median OS vs chemo in metastatic PDAC (13.2 vs 6.7 mo, HR 0.40; RAS G12 HR 0.40, P=5.9×10⁻¹⁰), the hardest tumor in oncology, with simultaneous NEJM publication. This validates the direct-RAS franchise that dominated [JPM 2026 deal chatter](../jpm-2026/themes.md) and the KRAS-G12D/pan-RAS programs profiled at [AACR 2026](../aacr-2026/index.md) — moving the class from preclinical promise and G12C niche into broad, plenary-grade clinical reality.

## 2. The PD-1×VEGF bispecific class arrives

[HARMONi-6](trials/harmoni-6.md) (LBA4) delivered the **first OS-relevant win for a PD-1×VEGF bispecific**: ivonescimab + chemo beat tislelizumab + chemo in 1L squamous NSCLC (median OS 27.89 vs 23.69 mo) — an active-comparator, head-to-head-vs-PD-1 design, not a placebo bar. This is the clinical readout behind the **AbbVie + RemeGen $5.6B PD-1×VEGF license** that was the [biggest single JPM 2026 headline](../jpm-2026/themes.md), and it cements the bispecific backbone as the next 1L IO platform.

## 3. Perioperative and adjuvant targeting expands — but selection matters

Moving active drugs earlier paid off when the target was right and failed when it wasn't. [PROTEUS](trials/proteus.md) (LBA1) made perioperative apalutamide + ADT a new SOC in high-risk localized prostate (MFS HR 0.80; pCR/MRD 8.9% vs 1.0%). [LIBRETTO-432](trials/libretto-432.md) (LBA3) cut recurrence/death 83% with adjuvant selpercatinib in RET+ NSCLC — the first adjuvant RET TKI. [KN026-004](trials/kn026-004.md) lifted neoadjuvant tpCR in HER2+ early breast cancer, and [lidERA](trials/lidera-giredestrant-adjuvant.md) cut iDFS risk 30% with adjuvant giredestrant. The counter-example: [ANVIL/ALCHEMIST EA5142](trials/alchemist-ea5142.md) — adjuvant nivolumab gave **no DFS benefit** in EGFR/ALK-WT resected NSCLC (HR 0.97), a reminder that unselected adjuvant IO has hit its ceiling.

## 4. ADCs and the durability question

The Trop-2 ADC story matured from response rate to durability. [ASCENT-04](trials/ascent-04.md) (LBA1000) showed sacituzumab govitecan + pembro beat chemo + pembro in 1L PD-L1+ metastatic TNBC (PFS 11.2 vs 7.8 mo, HR 0.65), with the ASCO 2026 update adding PFS2/subsequent-therapy data and **benefit consistent across every biomarker subgroup** including low Trop-2 (Trop-2 Q4 HR 0.57). [DESTINY-Breast09](trials/destiny-breast09-response-durability.md) reinforced the durability theme (median CR treatment duration 28.0 mo), while novel ADC platforms surfaced behind it: the Nectin-4 topo-I ADC [LY4052031](trials/nexus-01-ly4052031-nectin4-adc-urothelial.md), HER2 ADC [trastuzumab rezetecan](trials/horizon-crc01-trastuzumab-rezetecan-her2-crc.md) in CRC, and the first-in-class TROP2/HER3 bispecific ADC [JSKN016](trials/jskn016-trop2-her3-bispecific-adc.md).

## 5. Endocrine-backbone resistance: the next-gen SERD reckoning

The oral SERD class split. [SERENA-6](trials/serena-6.md) validated **ctDNA-guided early switching** to camizestrant at emergent ESR1m (PFS2 HR 0.52–0.63), and [evERA](trials/evera-giredestrant-everolimus.md) showed giredestrant + everolimus post-CDK4/6i (PFS2 19.0 vs 13.2 mo). But [persevERA](trials/persevera.md) **missed its primary endpoint** — giredestrant + palbociclib gave only a numerical, non-significant 1L PFS gain vs letrozole + palbociclib — even as the same molecule won in the adjuvant ([lidERA](trials/lidera-giredestrant-adjuvant.md)) and post-progression settings. The lesson: SERD benefit is real but **context- and biomarker-dependent** (ESR1m), not a universal 1L backbone swap.

## 6. China-origin assets dominate the LBA slate

A structural shift, not a footnote. China-originated molecules ran through nearly every track: ivonescimab ([HARMONi-6](trials/harmoni-6.md)), zanidatamab ([HERIZON-GEA-01](trials/herizon-gea-01.md)), sunvozertinib ([WU-KONG28](trials/wu-kong28.md), 1L monotherapy beating platinum doublet in EGFR exon20ins), the TROP2 ADC sac-TMT ([OptiTROP-Lung05](trials/optitrop-lung05.md)), anbenitamab ([KN026-004](trials/kn026-004.md)), satri-cel Claudin18.2 CAR-T ([CT041](trials/satri-cel-ct041-gastric-sequential.md)), olverembatinib ([CML](trials/olverembatinib-second-line-cml-cp.md)), the anti-CD5 nanobody CAR-T ([CONQUER](trials/conquer-anti-cd5-nanobody-car-t-tcell.md)), and JSKN016. This is the clinical-data manifestation of the "**China is the story**" thread from [JPM 2026](../jpm-2026/themes.md) — assets now reading out as practice-changing plenary data, not just licensing line-items.

## 7. Biomarker-matched wins crack open rare and hard tumors

Precision oncology kept clearing previously untreatable settings. [SARC041](trials/sarc041.md) (LBA2) was the **first positive phase 3 in dedifferentiated liposarcoma** — abemaciclib (CDK4 axis) extended PFS 9.7 vs 1.5 mo. [OptimUM-02](trials/optimum-02.md) gave 1L HLA-A*02:01-negative metastatic uveal melanoma a real option with darovasertib + crizotinib (PFS 6.9 vs 3.1 mo, ORR 37% vs 6%). [BREAKWATER](trials/breakwater.md) (EC + FOLFIRI in BRAF V600E mCRC, PFS 15.2 vs 8.3 mo) and [HERIZON-GEA-01](trials/herizon-gea-01.md) (HER2+ GEA) extended the matched-target logic into GI, and in heme [revumenib](trials/revumenib-aml.md) (KMT2Ar/NPM1m/NUP98r maintenance) and PRMT5 inhibitor [PRIMAVERA](trials/primavera-azd3470-prmt5-rr-hodgkin.md) pushed mutation- and epigenetic-target matching into late-line disease.

## 8. Bispecifics and CAR-T move to the front of heme

Off-the-shelf bispecifics started displacing standard regimens and even autologous CAR-T positioning. [MajesTEC-9](trials/majestec-9-teclistamab-rrmm.md) showed teclistamab **monotherapy beat PVd/Kd SOC** in RRMM (PFS HR 0.29), and [EPCORE DLBCL-1](trials/epcore-dlbcl-1-epcoritamab-rr-dlbcl.md) was the **first phase 3 of a CD3×CD20 bispecific monotherapy** to beat chemoimmunotherapy in R/R DLBCL. [frontMIND](trials/frontmind.md) brought the antibody tafasitamab into 1L high-risk DLBCL (PFS HR 0.75), [SUCCESSOR-2](trials/successor-2-mezigdomide-kd-rrmm.md) advanced the oral CELMoD mezigdomide, and the nanobody anti-CD5 CAR-T [CONQUER](trials/conquer-anti-cd5-nanobody-car-t-tcell.md) opened T-cell malignancies — collectively pulling redirected-T-cell therapy earlier in the heme treatment line.
