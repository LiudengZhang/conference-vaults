# Day 2 — Saturday May 30, 2026

**Source:** synthesized from public coverage and company releases (see [Sources](#sources)). The Plenary headliners (LBA1–5) land Sunday; Saturday's science was carried by the **frontMIND** lymphoma readout, the ceremonial **Opening Session**, and a clutch of practice-shaping breast and prostate datasets.

The 2026 ASCO Annual Meeting moved into its first full science day in Chicago. The marquee readout was **frontMIND** — the first positive frontline trial in a generation to beat R-CHOP in high-risk aggressive B-cell lymphoma — published simultaneously in *The Lancet*. The ceremonial spine of the day was the Opening Session, where President **Eric J. Small, MD, FASCO** delivered his address on the meeting's theme, *"The Science and Practice of Translation: Improving Cancer Outcomes Worldwide,"* alongside an address from NCI director **W. Anthony Letai, MD, PhD** and the **David Karnofsky Award Lecture** from **Nancy E. Davidson, MD, FASCO**. Around the ceremony, breast and GU oral sessions delivered new SERD, biparatopic-bispecific, and de-escalation data.

## Hematology — frontMIND headlines the day

[frontMIND](../trials/frontmind.md) was the day's defining readout: adding **tafasitamab + lenalidomide to R-CHOP** cut the risk of progression or death by **25%** vs R-CHOP alone in previously untreated high-intermediate/high-risk DLBCL or HGBL (HR 0.75, 95% CI 0.59–0.96, P=.019; 24-mo PFS 71.1% vs 62.9%; N=899). Interim OS was not yet significant (HR 0.85, P=.27). Investigators framed the triplet as a potential **new standard** — only the second phase 3 in ~25 years to improve a primary efficacy endpoint over R-CHOP in newly diagnosed DLBCL. Simultaneous publication in *The Lancet*.

## Breast — adjuvant SERD and a biparatopic bispecific

The oral SERD story continued with [lidERA](../trials/lidera-giredestrant-adjuvant.md): a Saturday menopausal-status subgroup analysis confirmed that adjuvant **giredestrant** improves invasive disease-free survival in ER+/HER2− early breast cancer **regardless of menopausal status** (overall iDFS HR 0.70; premenopausal HR 0.65, postmenopausal HR 0.74; 3-yr iDFS 92.4% vs 89.6%; N=4,170) — building on the 30% recurrence reduction first shared in late 2025.

[KN026-004 (Neo-Healer)](../trials/kn026-004.md) brought a HER2 biparatopic bispecific to the neoadjuvant setting (LBA660): **anbenitamab + nab-docetaxel ± carboplatin** beat THP on total pathologic complete response (tpCR 62.4% vs 51.2%; breast pCR 64.6% vs 55.0%; one-sided P=.0099; N~520).

A [NATALEE](../trials/natalee-ribociclib-gene-expression.md) translational analysis showed adjuvant **ribociclib** iDFS benefit holds across all PAM50 subtypes (HR range 0.42–0.77, no subtype×treatment interaction across 3,022 samples), hinting at gene-expression-guided risk refinement rather than patient selection.

## Genitourinary — de-escalating in exceptional responders

[A-DREAM](../trials/a-dream-intermittent-arpi-mhspc.md) (Alliance A032101) tested **planned ADT+ARPI interruption** in exceptional mHSPC responders: of 78 patients suspending therapy, **57.7% (45)** remained treatment-free at 18 months and **66.7% (52)** recovered testosterone (median 24.5 months off treatment) — supporting the feasibility of intermittent therapy and treatment holidays in deep responders.

## Other oral data reported Saturday

- [Olverembatinib 2L](../trials/olverembatinib-second-line-cml-cp.md) — second-line olverembatinib in CML-CP (N=42) reached 91.3% CCyR and 60.9% MMR by cycle 24.
- [PRIMAVERA](../trials/primavera-azd3470-prmt5-rr-hodgkin.md) — oral PRMT5 inhibitor AZD3470 in R/R classic Hodgkin lymphoma: 44% ORR overall, 63% ORR / 44% CR at 600 mg.
- [Satri-cel (CT041 Cohort 3)](../trials/satri-cel-ct041-gastric-sequential.md) — Claudin18.2 CAR-T sequential after 1L gastric/GEJ: ORR 100% in evaluable patients, median PFS-from-1L 20.9 mo at >4.5 yr follow-up.
