# Day 3 — Sunday, May 31

This was the load-bearing day of ASCO 2026: the Plenary Session, where five late-breaking abstracts (LBA1–LBA5) were chosen as the meeting's most consequential. The standing-ovation headline belonged to GI — **[RASolute 302](../trials/rasolute-302.md)**, the first positive phase 3 of a pan-RAS inhibitor in pancreatic cancer — but the Plenary spanned prostate, sarcoma, and two practice-shifting lung readouts, and the GI track delivered a second win in colorectal cancer alongside it.

## The Plenary headliner: pancreatic cancer finally moves

In second-line metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma, the oral RAS(ON) multi-selective inhibitor daraxonrasib nearly doubled median overall survival versus investigator's-choice chemotherapy — **13.2 vs 6.7 months (HR 0.40, P<.0001)** — with PFS 7.2 vs 3.6 months, ORR 31.6% vs 11.2%, and *fewer* grade ≥3 events (43.6% vs 57.5%). [RASolute 302](../trials/rasolute-302.md) (LBA5, N=500) is the first phase 3 RAS-inhibitor win in PDAC and was published simultaneously in *NEJM* — described from the podium as the most consequential shift in pancreatic treatment in over a decade.

## GU and sarcoma: two more Plenary firsts

[PROTEUS](../trials/proteus.md) (LBA1) established a new standard for high-risk localized prostate cancer: perioperative apalutamide + ADT cut metastasis/death risk 20% versus ADT alone (MFS HR 0.80), with pCR/MRD of 8.9% vs 1.0% and subsequent therapy delayed by 33 months (N=2,109).

[SARC041](../trials/sarc041.md) (LBA2) was the first-ever positive phase 3 in dedifferentiated liposarcoma: abemaciclib extended median PFS to **9.7 vs 1.5 months**, a ~62% reduction in progression/death versus placebo — a genuine new option in a track starved of them.

## Lung: an adjuvant first and a bispecific OS win

Two of the five Plenary slots went to thoracic oncology. [LIBRETTO-432](../trials/libretto-432.md) (LBA3) made adjuvant selpercatinib the first targeted option for resected RET fusion–positive NSCLC, reporting an **83% reduction in recurrence or death** (EFS) versus placebo. [HARMONi-6](../trials/harmoni-6.md) (LBA4) delivered the first overall-survival win for a PD-1×VEGF bispecific: ivonescimab + chemo beat tislelizumab + chemo in 1L squamous NSCLC, mOS **27.89 vs 23.69 months**. A subtype subanalysis of IMforte (Abstract 8014) also reported during the session, dissecting whether SCLC transcriptomic subtypes drive the maintenance lurbinectedin + atezolizumab benefit.

## GI, beyond the Plenary: BREAKWATER in BRAF mCRC

The day's GI momentum carried into the late-breaking oral sessions. [BREAKWATER](../trials/breakwater.md) showed first-line encorafenib + cetuximab + FOLFIRI nearly doubled PFS versus FOLFIRI ± bevacizumab in BRAF V600E mCRC — **15.2 vs 8.3 months (HR 0.44, P=.0002)** — with ORR 64.4% vs 39.2% and OS not reached vs 20.3 months (HR 0.56). The regimen already carries full FDA approval in this setting.

## Breast: a confirmatory 1L TNBC signal

In 1L PD-L1+ (CPS≥10) metastatic triple-negative breast cancer, the [ASCENT-04](../trials/ascent-04.md) update confirmed sacituzumab govitecan + pembrolizumab over chemo + pembro (PFS 11.2 vs 7.8 months, HR 0.65), with benefit holding across biomarker subgroups — deepest in high Trop-2 (Q4 HR 0.57) and retained in tBRCA wild-type and HER2 IHC 0 disease.

---

**Bottom line:** Sunday delivered the meeting's defining moment — a pan-RAS inhibitor doubling survival in pancreatic cancer — wrapped in a Plenary that also reset standards in prostate, liposarcoma, and RET-positive lung, with a colorectal win for good measure.
