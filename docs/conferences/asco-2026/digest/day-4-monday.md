# Day 4 — Monday, June 1

After Sunday's Plenary fireworks, Monday was the day the disease-specific oral sessions delivered their own headliners. The standout was a rare-tumor win — **[OptimUM-02](../trials/optimum-02.md)**, the first phase 3 to establish a systemic standard in HLA-A*02:01–negative metastatic uveal melanoma — but the day was defined as much by two instructive negatives in lung cancer (**[ANVIL/ALCHEMIST](../trials/alchemist-ea5142.md)** and **[TRIPLEX](../trials/triplex.md)**) and a GI HER2 win that reshapes first-line gastroesophageal treatment.

## Melanoma: a first standard for uveal melanoma

In the 9:00 a.m. Melanoma/Skin Cancers session, [OptimUM-02](../trials/optimum-02.md) gave a population with no approved systemic options a genuine one. In 1L HLA-A*02:01–negative metastatic uveal melanoma, darovasertib (PKC inhibitor) + crizotinib beat investigator's choice of therapy on median PFS — **6.9 vs 3.1 months (HR 0.42, 95% CI 0.30–0.59, p<0.0001)** — with ORR 37.1% vs 5.8% (p<0.0001) and an early OS trend favoring the combination. Grade 3/4 treatment-related events were manageable (diarrhea 10.0%, syncope 7.1%). The darovasertib NDA is already under FDA Real-Time Oncology Review.

## Lung: two negatives that teach

Monday's lung program was dominated by trials that did not hit — but informatively so. [ANVIL/ALCHEMIST (EA5142)](../trials/alchemist-ea5142.md), the largest US cooperative-group trial of adjuvant immunotherapy in NSCLC (N=935, Abstract 8000), reported that adjuvant nivolumab after surgery and chemotherapy did **not** improve disease-free survival in resected EGFR/ALK–wild-type disease — median DFS **71.3 vs 68.8 months (HR 0.97, 95% CI 0.81–1.17, P=.39)** — and missed even in the PD-L1≥50% subgroup (HR 0.86). The contrast with neoadjuvant CheckMate 816 sharpened the field's view that checkpoint blockade works best with an intact tumor present to prime T cells.

[TRIPLEX](../trials/triplex.md) (NCT05223647, NTNU/Grønberg), a phase 3 adding thoracic radiotherapy to 1L carboplatin/etoposide + durvalumab in extensive-stage SCLC, was terminated early on the recommendation of its Data Safety Committee for excess toxicity — a cautionary readout for layering concurrent radiation onto chemo-immunotherapy in ES-SCLC. Detailed efficacy figures remained embargoed at writing.

## GI: zanidatamab resets 1L HER2+ gastroesophageal cancer

[HERIZON-GEA-01](../trials/herizon-gea-01.md) brought a practice-changing win to first-line HER2+ locally advanced/metastatic gastroesophageal adenocarcinoma. Zanidatamab + chemo ± tislelizumab beat trastuzumab + chemo on both endpoints — PFS HR 0.63 (**median 12.4 vs 8.1 months, P<0.0001**) and OS HR 0.72 (**26.4 vs 19.2 months, P=0.0043**). A companion analysis (Abstract 4010) showed the triplet's benefit held across PD-L1 status, with PD-L1–negative triplet median OS reaching 29.7 months — addressing the field's worry that anti–PD-1 benefit concentrates in PD-L1–high disease.

## GU: an early signal in post-EV urothelial

In a first-in-human dose-escalation reported during the meeting, [NEXUS-01](../trials/nexus-01-ly4052031-nectin4-adc-urothelial.md) tested the Nectin-4 topoisomerase-I ADC LY4052031 in 36 patients with metastatic urothelial carcinoma after enfortumab vedotin: ORR 33% (47% at 3.6 mg/kg), DCR 75%, median DOR 7.4 months — though no responses followed a prior topo-I ADC, flagging a cross-resistance limit worth watching.

---

**Bottom line:** Monday's signal trial was [OptimUM-02](../trials/optimum-02.md), which finally puts a standard under HLA-A*02:01–negative uveal melanoma. But the day's real teaching came from lung — adjuvant nivolumab's flat [ANVIL](../trials/alchemist-ea5142.md) curve and [TRIPLEX](../trials/triplex.md)'s toxicity halt both narrowing where and how to deploy immunotherapy — while [HERIZON-GEA-01](../trials/herizon-gea-01.md) handed gastroesophageal cancer a new first-line backbone.
